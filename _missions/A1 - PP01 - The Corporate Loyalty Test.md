The crew has survived the EMP ambush and accepted Dr. [Dr Vivian V Kaine]({% link _people/Vivian Kaine.md %})'s ultimatum. Paid an upfront "bonus" of 1,000eb each and fully healed, the party must infiltrate a Continental Brands distribution hub in the Watson Development. Their objective is to secure the Bio-Organic Neurochip housed inside the "Portavendit" prototype. How they gather intel, breach the facility, and handle the prototype is entirely up to them.
