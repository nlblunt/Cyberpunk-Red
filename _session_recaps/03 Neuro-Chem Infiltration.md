# Neuro-Chem Infiltration
## Excerpt
After infiltrating Neuro-Chem Labs and bypassing Max’s personal security, the party uncovered a chilling digital trail. An email to the Piranhas confirmed that Sam’s abduction was no random act of violence, but a calculated betrayal orchestrated by his own manager. With the warehouse location secured, the team now moves toward the Haywood District to stage a rescue before the Piranhas follow through on Max’s permission to dispose of their captive.

## Session Summary
Following a tip from the Netrunner Neuro, the group discovered that Max, the manager of Neuro-Chem Labs, was using fraudulent shipping manifests to mask illegal payments to the Piranhas boostergang. The investigation nearly turned fatal at Sam Ishida's residence when a Piranha "disposal unit" arrived to scrub the scene. Though the party evacuated through a rear exit, the interior was purged with industrial acid; Pierce sustained chemical burns from the resulting toxic vapors while attempting to survey the damage.

Driven to find proof of Max’s involvement, the team executed a covert infiltration of the Neuro-Chem Labs facility. After navigating to the executive level, they breached Max’s private office. By leveraging personal Intel to bypass his workstation's password, they uncovered correspondence detailing a horrific betrayal: Max had coordinated Sam’s abduction, effectively signing a death warrant by granting the Piranhas total autonomy over the captive's fate. With the digital evidence secured, the party shifted their focus to a warehouse in the Haywood District, identified as Sam’s current—and potentially final—location.
