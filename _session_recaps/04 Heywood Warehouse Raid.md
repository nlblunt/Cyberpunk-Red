## 04 HEYWOOD WAREHOUSE RAID

### EXCERPT

The rescue mission for the missing corporate liaison escalated into a vertical infiltration when the crew staged a daring break-in through the warehouse skylights. Caught in a sudden crossfire after entering a storage room, the team had to rely on quick thinking and lethal precision to clear out the Piranha enforcers.

### SESSION SUMMARY

- **The Cardboard Diversion:** Upon arriving at the heavily fortified warehouse in the Heywood District, the crew engineered a quick distraction to draw the Piranhas away from the main entry point. Knuckles used a cardboard box to mask his approach, opening the front door to scout the interior, but confirmed that Sam was nowhere to be seen on the main floor.
    
- **The Skylight Ascent:** Shifting tactics to a less guarded vector, the crew moved around to the side of the industrial structure and spotted high-up windows. Piece quickly jury-rigged a functional grappling hook using a standard rope and an umbrella, allowing Knuckles to scale the wall and haul the rest of the party safely into the upper level.
    
- **Busted in the Store Room:** The team managed to slip through the windows and enter a dusty warehouse storage room. However, their infiltration was cut short when they opened the door to advance, immediately drawing the attention of two nearby Piranha gangers who marched over to investigate.
    
- **Roll for Initiative:** Acting with lethal reflexes, Knuckles silently choked out the first approaching ganger while Petra dropped the second with a devastating critical hit. Despite the quick takedowns, the distinct echo of the gunshot rang out through the facility, alerting the entire warehouse and forcing the crew into a desperate fight for survival.
