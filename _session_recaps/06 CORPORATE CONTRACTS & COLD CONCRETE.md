## 06 CORPORATE CONTRACTS & COLD CONCRETE

### EXCERPT

Staring down the barrel of corporate execution, the crew chose the lesser of two evils and accepted Dr. [Chief Executive Officer]({% link _people/Vivian Kaine.md %})'s grim job offer. Now operating under the megacorp's shadow, they've been tasked with a high-stakes asset recovery mission: liberating a proprietary processor chip from a Prototype Vendit machine nestled deep within a heavily guarded Continental Brands warehouse.

### SESSION SUMMARY

- **A Deal with the Devil:** With no real alternative on the table, the party officially accepted Dr. Kaine's "offer" of employment, trading their independence for their lives. Their inaugural assignment for their new corporate overseer is to infiltrate a Continental Brands facility and extract a specific, high-value processor chip from a Prototype Vendit machine.
    
- **R&R and Recon:** Before hitting the pavement, **Piece** took some much-needed downtime to fully heal up from the brutal chemical burns he sustained during Sam's rescue mission. The following day, the crew moved into position around the Continental Brands warehouse to conduct a thorough reconnaissance operation.
    
- **The Shift Change:** While monitoring the facility, the crew mapped out the operational patterns of the warehouse staff. However, any hopes of an easy night-shift break-in were dashed when **8 heavily armed guards** arrived on the scene to secure the perimeter just as the standard workers were clocking out.
    
- **The Underbelly Route:** Realizing a frontal assault against eight corporate guns would be suicide, Petra reached out to her reliable Netrunner contact, **Neuro**. Neuro successfully dug up the original structural blueprints for the warehouse, uncovering a potential blind spot: an old, forgotten city sewer system that mapped directly underneath the facility, offering a subterranean backdoor into the target zone.
